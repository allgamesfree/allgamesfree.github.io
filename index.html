About ALLGamesFree

ALL Games Free is an independent hub of free games you can play right in your browser, built around one idea: playing should be instant. Every title runs in the browser, so you can jump in from a laptop, tablet or phone without installing anything or creating an account.

Each game page includes a short guide covering the objective, controls, key mechanics and useful tips, plus a list of similar games to try next. We note which devices and controls each game supports, so you know before you start whether it plays best with a keyboard, a mouse or a touchscreen.

Your recently played, favorite and liked games are saved in your browser's GameVault, so you can get back to them in one click.
